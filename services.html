Our Services

Art Installations

As part of our main mission, we put up murals and install art instalations all across the Fox Valley. These art installations are often on behest of the city council, but we accept requests from the community as well.

If you have a request for a mural, please fill out our suggestion form.

Community Outreach

As part of our commitment to supporting out community, we collaborate with several volunteer organizations to provide lawn care, cleaning, transportation, and food for those who need it.

Restoration


We provide restoration of monuments, murals, and art installations across the Fox Valley. If the restoration is one we installed ourselves, we offer this free of charge, subject to our terms and conditions.
