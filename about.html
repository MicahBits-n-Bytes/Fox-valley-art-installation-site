Who are we?

We are a community art organization that turns public spaces into places people love. We believe art belongs to everyone, so we invite neighbors, students, and artists to create it side by side.

Background of Company

Our story began with one blank wall and a group of neighbors who wanted to brighten it up. That first mural drew more than 50 volunteers, and many had never held a paintbrush before.

Today we are a team of artists, teachers, and organizers. We create murals, art installations, workshops, and events that reflect the people who live in each neighborhood.

Our Mission

To connect people through creative public art that reflects their stories and makes shared spaces more welcoming.

Our Vision

A world where every neighborhood has art made by the community it belongs to.

Our Values

These ideas guide every project we take on.
